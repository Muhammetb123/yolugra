# Business rules

All rules are enforced by the backend; the app mirrors them for a better experience.

## Accounts

- Sign-in with a Turkmenistan mobile number (+993, 8 digits starting with 6 or 7) and a one-time
  SMS code generated by the backend. No passwords.
- One account is both driver and passenger.
- First and last name are mandatory before publishing or booking; bio and photo are optional.
- Changing the phone number requires a code sent to the new number. A number used by another
  account cannot be taken.

## Vehicles

- A user can own several vehicles. Make, model, colour and plate are required; year, body type,
  passenger seats (1–8, the driver's seat excluded) and photo are optional.
- Passengers see make, model and colour. The plate is shown only to the driver and to passengers
  whose booking is accepted (or completed).
- A vehicle used by a live trip cannot be deleted; deletion is soft so history keeps the car.

## Trips

- A route has 2–8 distinct places: departure, optional intermediate stops, destination. Each stop
  may have a meeting point, an estimated time, and a type: passengers may board, leave, or both.
- Departure in the future (up to 90 days); duration 15 min – 48 h; arrival is an estimate.
- Seats 1–8, at most the vehicle's passenger seats. Price per seat in TMT, optional, may be
  marked negotiable.
- **A driver cannot have two live trips whose times overlap**, including a 30-minute buffer
  (configurable). Cancelled, completed and expired trips do not count. Other drivers are not affected.
- Statuses: Published (`active`), Full, On the way (`in_progress`), Completed, Cancelled, Expired.
  Draft exists only in the app's step-by-step form until publication.
- After publication the route is fixed. Seats (not below accepted seats on any segment), price,
  description, conditions, comfort, meeting points and the vehicle can change; the date/time only
  while nobody has requested a seat. Accepted passengers are notified of changes.

## Bookings

- A passenger requests seats for a part of the route: a pickup stop that allows boarding, a later
  drop-off stop that allows leaving, seats, luggage and an optional message.
- Requests are pending until the driver accepts or declines. Pending requests do not hold seats,
  but a request that already does not fit is refused.
- Acceptance re-checks free seats on every segment of the requested part at that moment. Seats are
  counted per segment: passengers on different parts of the route can share a seat.
- A passenger has at most one pending or accepted booking per trip and cannot book their own trip.
- Transitions: pending → accepted | declined | cancelled | expired; accepted → cancelled | completed.
  Declined, cancelled, expired and completed are final. A declined request needs a new request.
- Requests that were not answered expire when the trip departs.

## Cancellation

| Who | What | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passenger | pending request | any time before it is answered |
| Passenger | accepted booking | before departure |
| Driver | pending or accepted booking | before departure |
| Driver | whole trip | before departure; every pending and accepted booking is cancelled |

Repeating a cancellation changes nothing. Seats return exactly once. The other side is notified.

## Completion

The driver can finish a trip after departure; otherwise the backend completes it 6 hours after the
planned arrival (expired if nobody was accepted). Accepted bookings become completed, which unlocks
reviews.

## Contact privacy

A phone number is returned only to the passenger and the driver of an accepted or completed
booking, by the backend, per request. Search results, trip details, public profiles and
notifications never contain phone numbers. Calls use the phone's dialer; there is no in-app chat.

## Reviews

After a completed booking the passenger rates the driver and the driver rates the passenger:
1–5 stars and an optional comment, once per booking and author, never oneself. The average and the
count are recomputed from all reviews in the same transaction.

## Notifications

Booking requested, accepted, declined, cancelled (by either side), expired; trip cancelled, updated,
starting soon, completed; review received. Each event is stored once per user (deduplicated).
